WEEKLY REVISION DECK
🧭 Reader's Roadmap: 3-Step Mastery Architecture
1️⃣ Bilingual Decoding
Inspect the high-contrast Devanagari meaning alongside the concise English definition to anchor native comprehension before memorising synonyms.
2️⃣ Memory Hook Retention
Leverage the dedicated Memory Hook column to establish conceptual, etymological, or rhyming associations for rapid active recall.
3️⃣ Lexical Boundaries
Review the 3 synonyms and 3 antonyms per row to grasp subtle nuance shifts and avoid common traps in reading comprehension.
⚡ High-Yield Spotlight Target: Items flagged with ⚡ represent high-frequency, complex exam vocabulary scored by lexical rarity. Prioritise these during rapid last-minute revision passes.
🗂️ Universal Categorical Structure (6 Knowledge Pillars)
1. Editorial Vocab
Core contextual lexicons with sentiment connotations and POS tags.
2. One-Word Subs
Single-term replacements for complex descriptions and conceptual definitions.
3. Fixed Prepositions
Crucial prepositional dependencies frequently tested in error-spotting.
4. Phrasal Verbs
Multi-word verb combinations, verbal phrases, and contextual meanings.
5. Idioms & Phrases
Metaphorical expressions and editorial figures of speech.
6. Foreign Words
Latin, French, and loanword legal terms routinely used in editorial columns.

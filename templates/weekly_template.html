About This Series

Weekly Compilation Guide

Welcome to the Ez Editorialś Weekly Vocab Lab. This magazine compiles the most critical editorials from Monday to Saturday into a single, high-yield revision document.

It is designed to help you quickly review context-based vocabulary, phrasal verbs, and idioms extracted directly from top newspapers.

Vocabulary Sections

Inside each article breakdown, words are categorized for targeted learning:

  • Core Vocabulary: Essential words with context and bilingual meanings.
  • Phrasal Verbs & Idioms: High-frequency phrases for reading comprehension.
  • One-Word Substitutions: Direct replacements for lengthy phrases.

📌 About The Weekly Compilation

This edition aggregates every high-yield vocabulary word, phrase, idiom, and grammatical expression curated from Monday to Saturday editorials. Rather than reviewing individual articles, all terms are unified into Universal Categorical Sections for fast, systematic revision.

🗂️ Universal Categorical Structure

1. Editorial Vocabulary Core contextual words with bilingual definitions and connotations.
2. One-Word Substitutions Single-term replacements for complex conceptual definitions.
3. Fixed Prepositions Crucial prepositional dependencies frequently tested in competitive exams.
4. Phrasal Verbs & Idioms Multi-word expressions, verbal phrases, and metaphorical sayings.
